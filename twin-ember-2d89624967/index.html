DEPTH 03 · THE STUDIO

Rekindled since the 8-bit era.

An ember is what's left when the fire seems out: a coal that keeps glowing until someone gives it air.

We grew up with pixels, cartridges and long nights behind the screen. Life happened, the fire never went out. Now, around forty, we're blowing on it together. Two embers, one fire.

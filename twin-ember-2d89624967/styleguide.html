BRAND GUIDE · VERSION 2

Two embers.
One fire.

Twin Ember V.O.F. is the digital studio of Roland and Patrick. Games first (Darklife is the first title), then anything digital that needs a spark. Everything in this style comes from one image: light in the dark.

An ember is what's left when the fire seems out: a coal that keeps glowing until someone gives it air. Grown up in the 8-bit era, rekindled together around forty.

01 · LOGO

The pixel flame

One flame from two halves: ember on the left, amber on the right. At the bottom two loose coals that together become one flame. The wordmark is Big Shoulders Display 800 in capitals, with "Ember" in ember (ember-deep on light).

04 · SHAPE AND SPACE

Almost square, on an 8 px grid

Flat, no shadows. Depth comes from the night steps and from layering. The motif: sparks as pixels rising from two sources.

Spacing

space-2 · 8Inside an element: icon beside label. Also the pixel size in patterns.
space-4 · 16Button and card padding; between related elements.
space-6 · 24Between blocks in a section; page margin on mobile.
space-10 · 40Between sections; page margin on desktop.

Radius

radius-sm · 2
The default: buttons, fields, tags.
radius-md · 6
Cards, panels, images.
radius-pill
Status dots and small badges only.

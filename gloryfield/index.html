The battle

Deploy, then watch it unfold

You see the enemy before the fight begins. Place your heroes and legions in your zone, pick a stance for each, and mind the ground: water, forest, bridges and Ash all change how a fight goes. Then the battle runs on its own. Your part is the plan, and the moment each hero unleashes a power.

Heroes

Five heroes, each their own way

Each hero leads troops into battle and fights in their own way. Level them up, promote them, unlock their six skills, and dress them in gear from grey to gold. Gear grows by levelling it with shards; the best colours are rare and earned.

The kingdom

Rebuild what was lost

Thirty buildings on a painted cliff, each plot waiting for you: farms, mills and mines, barracks and stables, a tavern, a forge and a watchtower. Put your people to work, keep them fed and happy, pass decrees in the Main Hall, and train the army that will carry your banners.

Training

Grow stronger off the battlefield

Every building has its own game. Shoot at Fenna's archery range with the wind in your face, strike the iron at the smithy at just the right moment, find the rune pairs that open the chest, play Twenty-One at the tavern, race the harvest and work the carpenter's bench. Each hero drills their own way. Good play gives gold, crystals, gear and experience.

The Ash

The land does not stay quiet

Ash creeps back into the freed lands. Send your heroes and legions on missions to cleanse it and rescue the people trapped there. Leave it lying and the Darkness grows: raids on the city, ambushes on your troops and bandits on the roads. Post legions and send out patrols from the Watchtower to keep the peace, but every soldier on guard is one less at home.

More in the game

There is always something going on

The travelling market

Traders come to town with goods, rare wares and gear. The more towns you free, the bigger the market; bandits on the roads make it smaller.

The Watch

The Watchtower sees signs it cannot place. Post legions and send out patrols to keep the Darkness down, the Ash away and your traders safe.

The Monument

Daily goals, growth goals and the great deeds of the campaign, each with its reward.

Four difficulties

Easy for the story, Medium as it is meant, Hard for commanders with richer loot, and God to set everything yourself.

Fair play

Earned, not bought

Gloryfield is free to play: you only pay for additional content, such as new campaigns. No loot boxes, and every chance in a loot chest is shown before you open it. The best gear comes from playing, not from the shop.

A single-player game with several saves, so you can play more than one kingdom side by side. Multiplayer will come if players ask for it.

Privacy Policy

Naberius Games · Effective 12 October 2026

In short

  • No account needed
  • Your kingdom stays on your phone
  • No ads
  • No tracking or analytics
  • Purchases only through Google Play
  • We sell no data

Who we are

Gloryfield is a strategy game for Android made by Naberius Games, an independent game studio in the Netherlands. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. If you have a question, write to info@naberiusgames.com.

What the game keeps on your device

The game saves your games on your own phone: up to four save slots, each with your kingdom, buildings, people, army, heroes, gear, campaign progress and the chosen difficulty, plus your settings (such as music on or off and how long the mission log is kept). We do not receive any of it. It is removed when you uninstall the game.

If your phone backs up its apps (Android backup), Android may include these saves in your own Google backup, so they come back on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; we have no access to it.

Purchases

Gloryfield is free to play. You can buy additional content, such as new campaigns, through Google Play. The payment is handled entirely by Google: we never see your name, card or payment details. The game only receives Google Play's confirmation of which item you bought, so it can unlock it and restore it on another phone. Read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

New campaigns are delivered as downloads by Google Play, in the same way as the app itself.

Advertising

Gloryfield shows no ads. If we ever add optional reward videos, we will update this policy first, and the game will ask for your consent before any ad is shown.

The guide on our website

The game's settings have a link to the Guide & FAQ on naberiusgames.com. It opens in your browser. Our website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep visitors' IP addresses in its logs for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, e-mail address or any account.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting or tracking of our own.
  • The game has no chat and no contact with other players.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Gloryfield is a fantasy strategy game with battles in a comic-book style, without blood. Because the game collects no personal information, has no ads, no chat and no accounts, it is safe to play at any age. Purchases need the approval of the Google account holder, and parents can require a password for every purchase in the Google Play settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, and you can object to its use. Naberius Games itself holds no personal data about you; your saves are only on your phone. For purchases, Google holds the payment data; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and change the date at the top.

Stacking Mad

Android · Coming soon

A block floats in from the side. Stop it right above your tower. Whatever sticks out gets sliced off, so every block gets a little harder. How high can you go? Grandpa Joop explains the game, cheers you on and dares you to beat his score.

One tap

Stop, slice, stack

Tap STOP when the block hangs right above your tower. Spot on is PERFECT, and three perfects in a row make your block grow again. Pick Easy, where the speed stays the same, or Hard, where it keeps getting faster.

Your coach

Grandpa Joop is watching

Joop, the stacking champion of the family, explains how it works, cheers at every milestone and keeps your last ten scores. Every day he sets a challenge just above what you usually reach. Beat him for a reward.

Goals and tools

A bomb for a bad cut

Reach your goals and earn tools: a pump that makes your tower wider, a snail that slows things down, a magnet, a life buoy, and a bomb or dynamite to blow away a few layers and start wide again. At 50 high you get a diamond block, at 75 a golden one drops in perfectly by itself.

Fair play

Free, and fair for kids

No purchases with real money. An ad only plays when you choose to watch a video, for an extra life, another go at your tower or double loot. Made for players of all ages, in English and Dutch, with a privacy-friendly setup for children.

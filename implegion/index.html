Your legion

One finger, a hundred imps

Steer your legion left and right. Run through the glowing +1 gates to grow it, shoot the weapon crates open for hellfire, flamethrowers, laser eyes, rockets or lightning, and stack perks like burning, freezing and critical hits. Six legions, each with its own weapon and colour.

The enemy

Every soldier is an ad trick

Barry Board hides behind a billboard. Penny Pop-up jumps out of a window when you least expect it. Lexi Likes throws hearts that charm your imps away, and the Tracking Drone follows you wherever you go. New soldiers show up as you get further, and each one has a story in the collection.

The village

Win back your home

Every boss you beat frees a part of the imp village from the ads. Rebuild Grandpa's cottage, the bakery, the forge and more: each building has its own job and its own painted inside. Decorate with ad scraps, pick a new look for the Community Hall, and watch the imps come back to life.

Fair play

A game that makes fun of ads, played the fair way

No purchases with real money, ever. Every chance in a chest is shown before you open it. An ad only plays when you choose to watch one for a reward, and the game never shows one you did not ask for. Made for players of all ages, with a privacy-friendly setup for children.

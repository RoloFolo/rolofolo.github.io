Privacy Policy

Naberius Games · Effective 30 September 2026

In short

  • No account needed
  • Your progress stays on your phone
  • Ads by Google AdMob
  • Personalized ads only with your consent, never for children
  • We sell no data

Who we are

ImpLegion is a mobile game made by Naberius Games, an independent game studio in the Netherlands. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. If you have a question, write to info@naberiusgames.com.

What the game keeps on your device

The game saves your level, coins, lives, shop upgrades, backpack, collection, chosen legion, daily rewards, settings (such as sound on or off) and the birth year you enter at the first start on your own device. This is stored only on your phone. We do not receive it, and it is removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

ImpLegion is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Some ads are optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward, such as double coins or another try after losing. From level 10 on, a full-screen ad can appear between levels, at most once every three won levels and never right after you lose.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and IP address,
  • information about your device and operating system,
  • how you interact with the ads (for example whether an ad was shown or tapped).

Google is responsible for this processing. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your consent and choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent the first time you open it, using Google's consent form. Ads are only personalized when you agree. You can refuse, and you can change your choice later from the game's privacy options.

On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings under Google > Ads.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, e-mail address or any account.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking of our own.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

ImpLegion is made for players of all ages, including children. The first time the game starts it asks for the player's birth year. This answer stays on the phone and is only used to choose which ads may be shown:

  • Under 13: ads are requested as directed to children. Google then shows only non-personalized ads suitable for all ages (rated G), and does not use the advertising ID or collect personal information for advertising. The consent form is not shown.
  • Under 16: ads are requested as for users under the age of consent, so they are not personalized.
  • 16 and older: the consent choices described above apply.

The game has no chat, no accounts and no purchases with real money. We follow the Google Play Families policy. If you believe information about a child was collected through the game's ads, contact us and we will help you have it removed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, and you can object to its use. Naberius Games itself does not hold personal data about you. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the choices above or Google's own tools at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
